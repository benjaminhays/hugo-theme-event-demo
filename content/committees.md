# Committees

#### GA: Women's Rights Under Theocratic Governments (SOCHUM)

A session of the United Nations General Assembly's Third Committee: Social, Humanitarian, & Cultural Issues (SOCHUM). This conference aims to address issues relating to women's rights within the context of theocratic governments. What challenges do women face in accessing education, healthcare, and economic opportunities in theocratic states, and what can the UN do to improve it?

#### Specialized: The Scramble for Antarctica

The Antarctica Treaty has expired, and a specialized UN Committee has been formed from the nations most affected. These delegates are being tasked with deciding whether to place the continent under international control or fought over by nation-states.

#### Crisis: The Emu War

Migratory Emus have plagued the Australian continent. Farmers are losing their livelihoods and the nation's economy is taking a sharp decline. As elected politicians, military officials, and representatives of various stakeholder groups, the crisis committee will fight to ensure the optimal and least costly conclusion to the Emu overpopulation concerns of Western Australia, 1932.
